A late recommendation letter can sink a complete application. 📨

How to ask so your referee says yes, and submits on time:

1. Ask three to four weeks early
2. Choose someone who knows your work
3. Send your CV, transcript and the scholarship link
4. Explain how and when to submit
5. Remind them a week before, then say thanks
