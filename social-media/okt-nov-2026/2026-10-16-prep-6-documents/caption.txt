Scholarships open and close fast. The documents take weeks. 📄

Prepare these six before the next one opens:

1. A valid passport
2. Transcripts and certificates (certified copies)
3. An updated CV
4. A motivation letter
5. Two or three recommendation letters
6. Proof of English
