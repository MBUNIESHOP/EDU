“I can't apply, I don't have IELTS.” Check first. 🗣️

Four ways scholarships and universities accept proof of English:

1. IELTS Academic
2. TOEFL iBT
3. Duolingo English Test (accepted by some, not all)
4. A medium of instruction letter from your university

Requirements differ by university and by scholarship, so check the rules of each programme before you pay for a test.
