Four funded scholarships, four deadlines between now and January. ⏳

1. Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships: PhD or research in Switzerland. Deadline for Tanzania is 10 November 2026.
2. Gates Cambridge: postgraduate study at Cambridge. 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027, depending on the course.
3. Commonwealth Shared Scholarships: a one-year Master's in the UK. Scheduled to open in early November and close in mid-December 2026.
4. Erasmus Mundus: a joint Master's in Europe. Each programme sets its own deadline, mostly October to January.

Always confirm dates on the official website before you apply.
