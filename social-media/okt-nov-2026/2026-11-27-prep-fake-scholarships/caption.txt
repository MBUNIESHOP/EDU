“Pay a fee and your scholarship is guaranteed.” It is not. 🚩

Five signs a scholarship offer is fake:

1. They sell a guaranteed place
2. There is no official website
3. They promise you will win
4. They push you to pay fast
5. You cannot find it on the government or university website

Always apply on the official website of the institution.
