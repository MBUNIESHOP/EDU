A Bachelor's, Master's or PhD in Türkiye, with tuition, housing and flights covered. 🇹🇷

Türkiye Bursları is the Turkish government scholarship.

Age limits, counted on 1 January of the application year:
✅ Bachelor's: under 21
✅ Master's: under 30
✅ PhD: under 35

It covers tuition, a monthly stipend, accommodation, return flights and one year of Turkish language study.

The last cycle ran from 10 January to 20 February. The 2027 dates are not announced yet, so prepare your documents now.

Apply only on turkiyeburslari.gov.tr. It is free, and you do not need an agent.
