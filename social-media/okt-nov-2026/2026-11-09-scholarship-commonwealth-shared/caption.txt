A one-year Master's in the UK, with tuition, flights and living costs covered. 🇬🇧

Commonwealth Shared Scholarships 2027/28 are for candidates from low and middle income Commonwealth countries, including Tanzania.

Who can apply:
✅ Citizens of an eligible Commonwealth country
✅ A first degree of at least upper second class standard
✅ You could not afford to study in the UK without the scholarship

Applications are scheduled to open in early November and close in mid-December 2026. You choose an eligible course, apply to the university for admission, and apply for the scholarship on the CSC online system.

Exact dates and the course list: cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk

More open scholarships: mbuniehub.com/scholarships
