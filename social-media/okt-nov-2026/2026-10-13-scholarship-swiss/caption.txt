PhD or research in Switzerland, with a monthly scholarship. 🇨🇭

The Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships 2027–2028 are open to Tanzanians for research fellowships and PhD study.

Who can apply:
✅ Master's degree completed by 31 July 2027
✅ Born after 31 December 1991
✅ A support letter from a supervisor at a Swiss university

It pays CHF 2,450 per month, health insurance, a flight allowance and a housing grant. Tuition fees are not covered.

Deadline for Tanzania: 10 November 2026, 23:59 Swiss time. You apply online on GO ESKAS. Official details: sbfi.admin.ch
