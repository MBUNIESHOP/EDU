A Master's in Europe, in more than one country, with a monthly scholarship. 🇪🇺

Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters are run by groups of universities. You study in at least two countries, and applicants from every country can apply.

The scholarship:
✅ EUR 1,400 per month for the length of the programme (up to 24 months)
✅ No tuition fees for scholarship holders

How to apply: pick a programme in the Erasmus Mundus Catalogue on the EU website and apply directly to it. Each programme sets its own deadline, mostly between October 2026 and January 2027.

Final-year students can apply if they graduate before the Master's starts.
