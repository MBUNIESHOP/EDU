A postgraduate degree at the University of Cambridge, fully funded. 🎓

The Gates Cambridge Scholarship is open to citizens of any country outside the UK who apply for a PhD, MPhil, MLitt, MRes or LLM.

It covers:
✅ The full university fee
✅ A living allowance
✅ Flights at the start and end of the course
✅ Visa and health surcharge costs

There is no separate form. You apply for your course on the Cambridge applicant portal and complete the Gates Cambridge section there.

Deadline: 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027, depending on your course. Check yours at gatescambridge.org
