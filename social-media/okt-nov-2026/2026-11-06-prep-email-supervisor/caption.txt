Professors get dozens of “Dear Sir, I want a scholarship” emails. Most are never answered. 📧

An email a supervisor will read:

1. A clear subject line
2. Who you are, in two lines
3. Why their work
4. Your idea, in three or four sentences
5. CV and transcript attached, and one clear question

Keep it under 200 words.
