A Bachelor's, Master's or PhD in Hungary, tuition-free. 🇭🇺

Stipendium Hungaricum is the Hungarian government scholarship, and Tanzania is one of the partner countries.

It covers:
✅ Tuition-free study
✅ A monthly stipend
✅ Accommodation support
✅ Medical insurance

The last cycle opened in mid-November and closed on 15 January. The 2027/28 call is expected on a similar timeline, so this is the time to prepare your documents.

You apply online, and Tanzania's sending partner nominates candidates. Check the dates and the Partners page at stipendiumhungaricum.hu
